About the auto blocks & notifications

Dashed items (like workouts and the grocery run) are auto-generated from your usual weekly rhythm, not stored individually — adjust the pattern in Week view settings rather than deleting single instances.

Notifications fire reliably while this app is open, or recently opened on this device (foreground + background-tab). They won't fire if the app has been fully closed for a long stretch — that needs a small paid Firebase upgrade (Cloud Functions) to send true closed-app push. Ask if you ever want that added.
