How accurate nutrition tracking works

The model behind the interface
1
One standardized nutrition basisEvery food stores nutrients for a known reference amount such as 100 g, 100 ml, or one item.
2
Unit-aware conversionGrams, ounces, cups, tablespoons, teaspoons, pieces, and servings convert through the ingredient’s density or unit weight.
3
Recipe scalingIngredient totals are summed, then divided by the recipe’s serving count. A logged half-serving is multiplied by 0.5.
4
Editable source dataLabels and brands vary. Custom foods let you enter the exact package values you use.
