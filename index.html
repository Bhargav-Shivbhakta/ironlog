Your own animated Today banner — picking one here only changes yours, never the other profile's. Click a banner to activate it. The checkbox in the corner of each one controls whether Shuffle/rotation is allowed to pick it — uncheck any you don't want showing up on their own.

Today page

Schedule widget

The Today's Schedule widget can automatically scroll down to your current time block as the day goes on. Off by default — turn it on if you want that; if the dashboard has ever jumped around on its own, this is what was doing it.

Hands-free

Voice control

Say "Hey Jarvis" to open an app, add a task, or hear a quick summary of your day — see the full list of things it understands below. It only listens while this is turned on and the Hub is open in this browser; your browser will show a mic-in-use indicator the whole time. Voice audio is processed by your browser's own speech service (not stored by this app) to turn it into text.

Try: "Hey Jarvis, open gym" · "add a task to buy milk" · "add a note that the car needs gas" · "mark trash as done" · "switch to Bhargav" / "switch to Anusha" · "dark mode" / "light mode" · "what's next" · "how many tasks do I have" · "what time is it" · "repeat that" · "go to today" · "what can you do"
